English Basics — Part 3

Your day, your world

Part 3 moves into talking about everyday life: the rooms you haven't covered yet, the calendar and weather, how you spend your free time, your routine, how you feel, what people do for work, bigger numbers, and introducing yourself properly.

What to focus on: Several tasks here ask you to reason a little, not just recall a word (e.g. "how do you feel before a trip?"). Read the whole question, picture the situation, then answer — that's closer to how you'll actually use English in conversation.
